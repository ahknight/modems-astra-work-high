# Development journal

## 2026-11-20 · Opening the line

Started from the empty public repository. Adopted a build-free, local-first publication for GitHub Pages. Eight listening chapters share one transport. The short annotated signal excerpts and the historical narrative will identify their sources independently. No player may start on page load. The editorial and original illustrations and audio are CC BY-NC 4.0.

The project timeline follows interoperable signaling families. Dates refer to specific publications, products or decisions, and will be labeled where revisions or competing products complicate the chronology.

## 2026-11-20 · Sources and structure

Collected museum records, a contemporary BYTE notice, participant oral histories, standards edition records and signaling clauses. Kept the original and revised V.34 editions separate. The 56K chapter distinguishes the 1998 industry agreement from formal approval. The artifact photographs have independent rights records in assets/credits.json. All assets will be served from the repository.

The entry page introduces an acoustic coupler and a working Bell 103 monitor. Chapters will have independent static URLs and a common listening instrument. The narrative and references should survive disabled JavaScript. Keep the sender tracks separate so that the oscilloscope and spectrum can inspect the same samples the visitor hears.
